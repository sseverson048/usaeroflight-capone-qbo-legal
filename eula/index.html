2. What the Software does

USAeroFlight uses the Software for its own internal accounting of purchases made on its Capital One business credit card. The Software works with card transaction and statement information that USAeroFlight imports, and with receipts and invoices for those purchases. It matches charges to receipts, lets Authorized Users assign each purchase to accounting categories, and keeps a record of that review. The Software retrieves reference information, such as accounts, classes and existing expenses, from USAeroFlight's QuickBooks Online company. Where USAeroFlight authorizes it, the Software may be used to prepare or record related accounting entries, such as expenses, in USAeroFlight's own QuickBooks Online company.
