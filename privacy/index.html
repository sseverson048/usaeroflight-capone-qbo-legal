USAeroFlight Capital One–QuickBooks Integration Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Who operates the application

USAeroFlight LLC ("USAeroFlight", "we", "us") operates the USAeroFlight Capital One–QuickBooks Online Integration (the "Application"). It is internal business software that USAeroFlight and its authorized personnel use for USAeroFlight's own accounting of purchases made on its Capital One business credit card. It is not offered to the general public.

2. Information the application processes

The Application processes business and accounting information that USAeroFlight provides to it or that it retrieves from systems USAeroFlight has authorized it to connect to. Depending on the purchase, this may include:

  • card transaction and statement information that USAeroFlight imports, such as transaction dates, amounts, merchant names, descriptions, statement periods and the card's last four digits;
  • receipts and invoices for those purchases, such as scanned documents and photos, and the information on them, such as vendor names, item descriptions, quantities, prices, taxes, shipping and totals;
  • QuickBooks Online accounting records, such as accounts, classes, vendors and existing expenses; and
  • the results of USAeroFlight's review, such as which receipts were matched to which charges, the accounting categories assigned, approvals and any exceptions found.

The Application is not designed to collect full payment card numbers, card security codes or full bank account numbers. Receipts may occasionally show information about other people, such as a vendor's contact details. The Application uses such information only as part of the business record.

3. How the information is used

The information is used only for USAeroFlight's internal accounting. This includes matching card charges to receipts, reading text from receipts to help fill in purchase details, assigning purchases to accounting categories, producing records for USAeroFlight's review, keeping a history of what has been processed, and, where USAeroFlight authorizes it, recording related accounting entries, such as expenses, in USAeroFlight's own QuickBooks Online company.

4. We do not sell or rent information

USAeroFlight does not sell or rent information processed by the Application, and does not use it for advertising.

5. Who can access the information

Access is limited to USAeroFlight's authorized personnel who need it for accounting work, and to service providers needed to operate the Application. For example, Intuit (QuickBooks Online) processes data in its own services under its own terms and privacy policy. When an Authorized User chooses to have text read automatically from a receipt, that receipt document is sent to a third-party optical character recognition (OCR) service, which processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. USAeroFlight may also disclose information when required by law or to its professional advisors, such as accountants, as needed for its business.

6. Security

USAeroFlight takes reasonable steps to protect the information the Application processes. These include keeping access credentials out of the Application's source code and storing them separately from those of USAeroFlight's other applications, limiting who can use the Application, using encrypted (HTTPS) connections to QuickBooks Online and other online services, and storing working records on systems under USAeroFlight's control. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

The Application keeps accounting records, such as receipts, processing history and logs, for as long as USAeroFlight needs them for its accounting, recordkeeping, tax, audit and legal obligations. After that, they are deleted or securely disposed of. Records held by Capital One, QuickBooks Online and other service providers remain subject to those services' own retention practices.

8. Visitors to this website

This website only publishes the Application's legal documents. The Application does not intentionally collect information from casual visitors to this website. The site has no forms and does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses as part of providing that hosting service, under GitHub's own privacy practices.

9. Changes to this policy

USAeroFlight may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new Effective Date.

10. Questions

Questions about this Privacy Policy should be directed to USAeroFlight LLC management.
